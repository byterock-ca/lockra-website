Privacy Policy

Effective date — August 2026.

Information Collection

Lockra does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

Storage

All vault data is stored locally on the user's device and encrypted before storage.

Third-Party Services

Lockra does not use cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

User Responsibility

Users are solely responsible for keeping their master password, recovery key and vault backups secure.

Permissions

Lockra requests only the permissions required to provide its core features, such as biometric authentication and file access for importing, exporting, and sharing encrypted vault backups. These permissions are used only on the user's device and are never used to collect personal information.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the application, please contact us at admin@byterock.ca.

Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
